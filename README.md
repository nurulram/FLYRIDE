# FLYRIDE
A future system that helps people to decide whether they should use road cars or flying cars.
Title: FlyRide


By 2050, it is believed that future vehicles may operate in two modes of transportation, which are ground mode and flight mode. In future, cars will be still widely used, but roads and air routes may be become more congested as the number of vehichles keep increasing. We are focusing on a smart system that helping users whether they should use ground or flight mode by analysing the travel conditions such as travelling distance, weather, congestion level , air and ground route availability. It is expected to help the users to choose the best mode of transportation for them to reach their destination safely and improve the traffic flow in the future.
